About Splitify

Discover the story behind the platform and its creator

Next-Gen Fintech

Split expenses. Effortlessly.

Splitify is a modern, premium web platform crafted to eliminate the stress of manual financial sharing. We combine glassmorphic design and intuitive workflows to make tracking, splitting, and settling bills seamless for friend circles, roommates, and travel groups.

Our Mission & Vision

To build a frictionless financial hub where trust and transparency are automated. We believe social debt shouldn't compromise friendships, which is why we created a tool that makes tracking instant and settlements completely clear.

Key Core Features:

  • ✦ Real-time expense distributions and automated ledger balances
  • ✦ Premium, immersive dark-first aesthetic tailored for financial monitoring
  • ✦ Intelligent settlement optimization algorithms to minimize group transfers
